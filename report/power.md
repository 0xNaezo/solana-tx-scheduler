# Power analysis (synthetic windows)

Generated by `solana-tx-scheduler simulate --reps 300 --txs-per-window 10 --validators 23`.

- Each window: 10 transactions on the priority ladder, design cells cycled as in `run` (send order reverse/random × pauses 0, 10, 50 ms × shared/distinct payer).
- A transaction reaches the leader at its send time plus Gaussian jitter (sd 1 ms), so pause-0 transactions arrive in random order. It lands with p=0.8, in the slot its arrival falls into: slots are 213 ms long at a random phase, so a 50 ms-pause batch spans 3 slots.
- Within a slot, the order is the rule's score plus Gaussian noise (sd on a 0..1 priority scale; adjacent ladder steps are 0.11 apart). FIFO means arrival order at the leader.
- p-values from 2000 permutations (`analyze` uses 10000). Holm runs over 2 × 23 tests, with the other validators showing no effect, which is the worst case.
- A cell is the share of runs where the pre-registered classifier returned the true label. For batching, that is the batched label with a fitted batch length within a factor of 1.5 of the true one. For noise, it is 1 − false-rule rate.
- Mean D is the estimate at the largest window count. Where it sits near the 0.8 / 0.2 thresholds the truth itself is borderline, and a "mixed" label reports lower strictness rather than missing the direction.
